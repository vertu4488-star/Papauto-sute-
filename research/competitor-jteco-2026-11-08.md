## Actual website layout
- The homepage separates Japan, Korea, China (new/used), motorcycles and equipment.
- Public completed-auction statistics has make, model, body, auction, auction grade, years, engine volume, mileage and lot number filters.
- The displayed sold-lot cards include auction venue, lot identifier, date, original start price, auction average, sale price in JPY, sell status, a ruble equivalent / indicative Russia delivery price, and a 'show expenses' control.
- Its public listing for Daihatsu Hijet Cargo SPECIAL CLEAN HIGH ROOF 2019, KCAA M Kyushu #6001, dated 2026-09-16 reports 50,000 km, grade 3.5, starting 80,000 JPY and sale 353,000 JPY. This is JTECO's displayed claim, not independently audited auction proof.
- Auctions online and sold archives are separate sections.

## Suggested PapAuto adaptations
1. Preserve **our own** site design and customer route; no competitor outbound links.
2. For independently verified real lots only, show venue/date/lot ID, starting price, average sale price if sourced, actual sale price, grade, mileage, and genuine auction sheet if rights permit.
3. For illustrative model photography (CC0), show reference model with example-cost calculation; do not suggest this is a specific sold vehicle.
4. Show C&F in JPY; duty on one line EUR, USD, RUB; SBKTS plus included expenses as a single expandable package; storage, commission, Vladivostok cost, and truck delivery to Novosibirsk as separate cost rows.
5. Keep photo files first-party; require confirmed image license, record source/licensing in research only.
6. Keep price figures auditable, distinguish model examples from real sold lots, avoid mixing records/photos.
